Personal Assistant

This is my personal, single-user AI assistant project. It connects to my own Gmail and Google Sheets account via OAuth to help with everyday tasks such as searching and summarising email, drafting replies, and reading or updating spreadsheets - for my own use only.

This is not a public product or service, and it is not available for other users to sign up for or authorize.
