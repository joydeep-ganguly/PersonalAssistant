Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Personal Assistant is a single-user project built and operated by its owner for personal use only. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the owner authorizes it against their own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the owner's own Google account authorization, the application can read, search, and send email via the Gmail API, and read and modify spreadsheets via the Google Sheets API, strictly to carry out tasks the owner requests of their own assistant.

How data is used and stored

Data retrieved from Gmail or Sheets is used only to generate a response to the owner's own request, within the owner's own private deployment of this application. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. OAuth tokens are stored only within the owner's own private cloud infrastructure, never shared externally.

Third-party access

No one other than the application owner has access to this deployment or the data it processes.
